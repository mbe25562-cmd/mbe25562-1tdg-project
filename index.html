Fashion club

Photo by Toa Heftiba on Unsplash

The fashion club is a club founded by textiles students to make a space for people to explore different styles and share a passion about it,find trending and creative designs to level up your fashion, share cultural fashion, and gain skills to be creative.After considering our resources the students were able to convince their textiles teacher Mrs Marr and we decided to created a fashion club for students who are interest in fashion and design.It is Founded by Marie Bende and Matdy Phongsavath.
