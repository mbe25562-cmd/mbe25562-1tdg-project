Fashion club

The fashion club is a club founded by textiles students based on the talents that is limited to just class projects when students have much more talent to offer. After considering our resources the students were able to convince their textiles teacher Mrs Marr and we decided to created a fashion club for students who are interest in fashion and design.It is Founded by Marie Bende and Matdy Phongsavath.
